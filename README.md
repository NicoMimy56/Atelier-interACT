# Atelier — Fundamentals of HCI

Group project for the course **Fundamentals of Human-Computer Interaction**  
Politecnico di Milano · a.y. 2025/26 · Course theme: **Living with AI**

---

## The problem

When people make **physical parts** (prototypes, prints, assemblies, maquettes, details), they increasingly use AI assistants (ChatGPT and similar) to figure out *how to do it*, *size it*, or *assemble it*.

Often the AI returns **closed instructions**: you follow them, the result may even work, but you never build a mental model of constraints, consequences, and trade-offs. The risk is **deskilling** and poorly informed decisions.

## Mission

Explore how to restore **understanding, alternatives, uncertainty, and responsibility** when AI enters material making — before the commit (print, cut, assemble…).

> The technological form (web, tablet on the bench, XR…) is **not decided upfront**: it will emerge from needfinding.

## Users

Students and makers who design and build physical parts:

- student teams (Formula SAE, motorcycle, rocketry, aeromodels, robotics, space…)
- Design / Engineering / Architecture with prototypes or maquettes
- lab and makerspace users (e.g. Polifactory)

## Needfinding

We are collecting:

1. **Form** — screener on AI use with physical parts  
2. **Interviews** (15–20 min) — real behaviors, not solution validation  

Question focus: what people ask the AI, how literally they follow it, what they don’t understand but do anyway, how they verify, what they would change.
